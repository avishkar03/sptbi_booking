About Us

Science and Technology Park - Technology Business Incubator (SP-TBI) is a premier incubation center established to nurture innovative startups and foster entrepreneurship in the technology sector.

Located at Bhavan's Campus, Andheri (West), we provide state-of-the-art infrastructure and comprehensive support to help transform innovative ideas into successful business ventures. Our focus spans across various technological domains including IoT, AI/ML, Robotics, Clean Energy, and Healthcare.

With a track record of incubating over 50+ successful startups, our ecosystem offers entrepreneurs access to cutting-edge laboratories, dedicated workspace, expert mentorship, funding opportunities, and invaluable industry connections. We are committed to fostering innovation and contributing to India's startup ecosystem.
